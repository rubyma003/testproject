# testproject# FlightPath (co2 flight calculator)

An easy-to-use web application that shows how much pollution a plane creates when flying from one airport to another. Users can select their starting city and destination to see their flight route and a clear estimate of their trip's carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions, compared against everyday activities like driving a car or using home electricity.

## How It Works Step by Step

1. **Select Airports:** Users pick their starting departure airport and destination airport from the interface.
2. **Calculate Route & Emissions:** The app computes the flight path and estimates the total CO2 output produced by the aircraft.
3. **Compare Impact:** The website translates the flight's emissions into relatable everyday activities (like driving a car or home energy usage) so travelers instantly understand their environmental footprint.
